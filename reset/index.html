Reset Notes

Your notes are not touched. This clears the app's cached program files and its service worker, then Notes downloads a fresh copy next time you open it. Note content lives in the database and in local storage, neither of which this page goes near.

This page lives outside the app's scope on purpose, so it can never be the stale copy it exists to replace. On iOS it has to be reachable from inside the installed app — clearing website data in Safari does not touch the installed app's container.
